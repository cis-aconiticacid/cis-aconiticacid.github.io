Batched entropic optimal transport on GPUs

Under review. A Sinkhorn solver for workloads made of many small, independent OT problems, where each problem stops as soon as it has converged instead of waiting for the slowest one in the batch. Paper under double-blind review; I’ll add the paper and code here after the decision.

Decoding images from EEG

Ongoing. Retrieving the image a person saw from a single EEG trial, scored by nearest-neighbour search in a DINO embedding space (THINGS-EEG2). My focus is cross-subject alignment: per-subject encoders trained to map into a shared space, then a subject-agnostic encoder that works on people it hasn’t seen. I’m also looking at shortcuts in EEG benchmarks that let models score well without decoding the stimulus.

NeurIPS 2026 EEG/EMG Foundation Challenge, EEG-to-Image track · code

Masked modeling in frequency space

Exploring. Whether masked training on natural images works when the masking happens in the frequency domain, and what representation keeps both object boundaries and frequency orientation intact.

Low-rank predictors for JEPA world models

Archived. Spectral and low-rank variants of the JEPA predictor. A rank-4 factored predictor matched the baseline with about a third fewer parameters. Includes experiments on representation geometry and spectral regularization. code

Hilbert-metric dynamics on cones

Archived. Using the Hilbert projective metric and Birkhoff contraction to study iterations on positive cones: gradient flows, embeddings, and PageRank under noise. nonlinear Perron–Frobenius · gradient flow · noised PageRank

Lattice-based cryptography

Past. A Directed Reading Program project on lattice-based cryptography, plus a toy study of whether sparse Transformers can learn LWE decryption and SWIFFT arithmetic. code
